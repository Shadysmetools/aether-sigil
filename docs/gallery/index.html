Emberwick

A tactics game where every creature lives in a lantern, and light is the territory you fight over.

How a battle looks

Light spreads, Wicks summon, Beacons fall

Your light is amber and rises from the bottom; the rival's Dusk light is violet and comes from the top. You can only summon where your light reaches. This loop plays a short battle.

These are concept sketches drawn in code to set the direction: shapes, colours, personality and motion. Final art would be painted by an artist you hire under a work-for-hire contract, so you own it outright. Every design here is original to Emberwick.
