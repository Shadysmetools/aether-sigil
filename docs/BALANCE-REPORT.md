# Aether Sigil: Balance Report

Generated by `docs/tools/aether_report.py` (core: `aether_core.py`, design constants: `aether_design.py`). Re-run with `python -X utf8 docs/tools/aether_report.py`. Every number below is computed by the script; the prose proposals are the analyst's.

Sources: `data/AETHER_SIGIL_MASTER_GAME_BIBLE.json` (primary), `data/1000_MONSTERS.csv`, `data/120_STAGES_AND_OPPONENTS.csv`, and `data/AETHER_SIGIL_GAME_BIBLE.pdf` (parsed with PyMuPDF). Helper tables are in `docs/data/`.

## 0. TL;DR

- **Rarity does nothing.** Rarity explains 0.2% of HP variance, 0.5% of attack, 0.0% of defense and 0.5% of power. A Legendary averages 3.10 power vs a Common's 2.80.
- **The damage floor dominates combat.** In 42.4% of all attacker/defender pairs, atk <= def, so the hit deals exactly 1. The median hits-to-KO is 41, and 58.8% of pairs need more than 20 hits.
- **Difficulty is flat and non-monotonic.** Spearman rho between stage order and mean roster power is +0.02 (+0.01 on the sum). On the mean there are 4 tower-to-tower inversions, and 54 of 108 within-tower steps go down. Boss stages average 17.4 roster power vs 16.9 for normal stages.
- **Two map rules can't work as written.** All 36 'secure 3 relic nodes' stages have exactly 1 relic tile. All 24 'random portal' stages have exactly 1 warp gate. 7 of 12 boss stages have no player Symbol tile.
- **Identity is locked by ID arithmetic.** Each element has exactly one role (16 element/role combos out of 16x8=128 possible). Range is a pure function of role (eta^2 = 1.00). Only 7 of 20 abilities are activatable, so 650 monsters have nothing to do with the Skill command.

## 1. Definitions and assumptions

**Power score.** `power = mean_j[max(1, atk - def_j)] x hp / mean_j[max(1, atk_j - def)] x (1 + 0.05*(move - mean_move) + 0.05*(range - mean_range)) / 100, with j over all 1000 monsters`. It equals expected damage dealt per hit times hits survived, with a +/-5% nudge per point of move/range. It uses the owner's damage formula against the whole population, so the 1-damage floor is priced in. Population: mean 2.692, sd 3.766, min 0.047, max 30.306. The distribution is heavy-tailed because `max(1, ...)` makes low-attack monsters nearly harmless.

**Stage difficulty** = the MEAN roster power (average power of the stage's enemy roster, as the brief defines it). The SUM ('threat') is reported next to it, because a squad of 4 must beat every enemy and roster size cycles 5/6/7. Inversions are listed for both.

**Coordinates.** `map_grid[row][col]`. Objective `tile` values are [row, col]. The JSON `player_spawns`/`enemy_spawns` fields disagree with the grid (see section 2), so **the grid is the source of truth** for every map metric.

**Walkability.** `obstacle` is blocked. Everything else is walkable: water/hazard/lava cost or hurt but do not block. `stone` is not in `map_legend`. It is treated as walkable-but-blocks-LOS (ruins/columns) in the main run, and every map metric was re-run with stone BLOCKED. Stages whose reachability or Symbol path changes between the two treatments: 1 (T08-L01: player->enemy Symbol 15->15, enemy->player Symbol 16->18, spawns connected True->True). Columns `walkable_pct_stone_blocks` and `chokepoints_stone_blocks` in `docs/data/map_analysis.csv`. Warp gates do NOT add BFS edges in the main numbers; `path_with_warps` adds them.

**Path length** = BFS steps (4-neighbour) from the nearest `player_spawn` tile to the `enemy_symbol` tile. **Chokepoints** = articulation vertices of the walkable 4-neighbour graph (tiles whose removal disconnects the board), counted in the spawn component. Bridge tiles are counted separately. **Capsule fairness** = for each `capsule_node`, BFS distance from the nearest player spawn minus the nearest enemy spawn, summed over the map's nodes. Negative means the player is closer. The grid's spawn template is itself asymmetric (section 9), so map-specific fairness is judged with the enemy spawns replaced by the point-mirror `(11-r, 11-c)` of the player spawns. A map is flagged UNFAIR when that corrected sum is beyond +/-2 either way, or when the Symbol-to-Symbol path lengths differ by 2 or more.


**Rules the data does not cover** (these definitions are reused in every spec below):

## 4. The damage floor

With `damage = max(1, atk - def)`, atk in [12,50] and def in [10,45]:

- 42.4% of all 1,000,000 attacker/defender pairs deal exactly 1 damage.
- Hits-to-KO: median 41. 7.8% of pairs KO in 5 or fewer hits, 58.8% need more than 20, and 48.1% need more than 50.
- Terrain and element multipliers barely matter against the floor: x1.30 of 1 is still 1.

Alternative formulas on the same population (round, min 1):

| formula | median hits-to-KO | pairs > 20 hits | pairs dealing 1 dmg | p90 hits-to-KO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| current: max(1, atk-def) | 41 | 58.8% | 44.9% | 176 |
| floor: max(0.25*atk, atk-def) | 16 | 36.8% | 0.0% | 37 |
| ratio: atk*atk/(atk+def) | 9 | 15.3% | 0.0% | 25 |
| ratio x0.6: 0.6*atk*atk/(atk+def) | 14 | 32.5% | 0.0% | 42 |
| ratio x1.5: 1.5*atk*atk/(atk+def) | 6 | 6.3% | 0.0% | 17 |

Recommendation: `dmg = max(1, round(1.5*atk*atk/(atk+def)))`. It is the ratio variant whose median hits-to-KO is closest to a 6-hit tactics target. Defense still matters but never trivialises a hit: median hits-to-KO 6, p90 17, 1-damage pairs 0.0%. If the owner wants to keep the subtraction, use the `max(0.25*atk, atk-def)` floor instead (median 16).

### Boss stages

Boss stages are exactly the 12 L10 stages. Mean threat: boss 17.42 vs non-boss 16.92. Mean roster power: boss 2.90 vs 2.82. ## 6. Opponent styles -> AI behaviour

10 distinct styles, each used 12 times (once per tower). Boss (L10) styles: {'rush attacker': 2, 'mobility specialist': 2, 'counter-attacker': 1, 'status-effect strategist': 1, 'trap engineer': 1, 'summoner': 1, 'long-range sniper': 1, 'defensive fortress': 1, 'terrain controller': 1, 'resource thief': 1}.

### defensive fortress (x12)

Stages: T01-L01, T02-L08, T03-L05, T04-L02, T05-L09, T06-L06, T07-L03, T08-L10, T09-L07, T10-L04, T11-L01, T12-L08

- **Target priority:** Lowest effective HP (hp/INC) unit inside its own half; ignores units outside its zone.
- **Positioning:** Holds the 3x3 ring around its Symbol and the nearest capsule node. Guardians stand on bridge or articulation tiles between the player and the Symbol. Never crosses the midline (row/col 6) before round 6.
- **Defend:** Defends when no target is in range after moving, or when HP < 50% and an ally can still attack.
- **Skill:** Uses shields/zone skills (Crystal Bloom, Lumen Ward, Gravity Well) on the turn an enemy first enters its half.
- **Tuning knob:** `aggro_radius = 4 tiles from own Symbol`

### trap engineer (x12)

Stages: T01-L02, T02-L09, T03-L06, T04-L03, T05-L10, T06-L07, T07-L04, T08-L01, T09-L08, T10-L05, T11-L02, T12-L09

- **Target priority:** The unit with the lowest move (it can't escape zones).
- **Positioning:** Pre-places zones and terrain on chokepoints (bridges, articulation tiles) in rounds 1-2, then kites at range 1 tile beyond the player's move+range.
- **Defend:** Rarely. Only when it is adjacent to 2+ enemies.
- **Skill:** Prioritises Gravity Well, Crystal Bloom and Magma Trail on chokepoints. Hidden-trap hazards (when the stage lists them) are placed on the 2 tiles nearest the player spawn path.
- **Tuning knob:** `trap_budget = 2 per round`

### mobility specialist (x12)

Stages: T01-L03, T02-L10, T03-L07, T04-L04, T05-L01, T06-L08, T07-L05, T08-L02, T09-L09, T10-L06, T11-L03, T12-L10

- **Target priority:** The isolated unit (no ally within 2 tiles), then backline (range >= 3).
- **Positioning:** Flanks: paths that avoid the player's threat area. Uses warp gates when that shortens the path by 3 or more. Ends turns out of the reach of 2+ enemies.
- **Defend:** Never. It Waits instead if it cannot attack safely.
- **Skill:** Movement skills first (Storm Dash, Phase Step, Astral Shift). Chases KO follow-ups.
- **Tuning knob:** `max_exposure = 1 attacker able to reach it`

### terrain controller (x12)

Stages: T01-L04, T02-L01, T03-L08, T04-L05, T05-L02, T06-L09, T07-L06, T08-L03, T09-L10, T10-L07, T11-L04, T12-L01

- **Target priority:** Units standing on terrain that grants them a bonus, then the closest unit.
- **Positioning:** Seeks elevation and family-matching tiles first (scores tiles: +3 elevation, +2 family match, -2 water, -3 hazard).
- **Defend:** When it stands on elevation and no target is in range.
- **Skill:** Skills that alter tiles or zones (Crystal Bloom, Magma Trail, Gravity Well). Tidal Pull is used to drag players onto water or hazard tiles.
- **Tuning knob:** `tile_score weights above`

### summoner (x12)

Stages: T01-L05, T02-L02, T03-L09, T04-L06, T05-L03, T06-L10, T07-L07, T08-L04, T09-L01, T10-L08, T11-L05, T12-L02

- **Target priority:** Whoever threatens its summons or capsules, then the nearest unit.
- **Positioning:** Stays 1 tile behind its front line. Contests capsule nodes and deploys the full roster as early as AP allows.
- **Defend:** When an enemy can reach it this round and it has already deployed.
- **Skill:** Spawn and copy skills (Crystal Bloom, Void Echo). Deploys a capsule every round it can.
- **Tuning knob:** `deploy_priority = max`

### counter-attacker (x12)

Stages: T01-L06, T02-L03, T03-L10, T04-L07, T05-L04, T06-L01, T07-L08, T08-L05, T09-L02, T10-L09, T11-L06, T12-L03

- **Target priority:** The last unit that damaged it or an ally; otherwise none.
- **Positioning:** Waits inside its own half until the player commits (a player unit ends a turn within 5 tiles), then converges on that unit.
- **Defend:** Defaults to Defend when nothing has attacked this round (bait).
- **Skill:** Reactive skills (Prism Guard, Metal Skin and Mist Veil are passive; actives used are Ember Mark on the aggressor and Lumen Ward on the hit ally).
- **Tuning knob:** `commit_radius = 5`

### resource thief (x12)

Stages: T01-L07, T02-L04, T03-L01, T04-L08, T05-L05, T06-L02, T07-L09, T08-L06, T09-L03, T10-L10, T11-L07, T12-L04

- **Target priority:** Units standing on capsule or relic nodes, then the player's Symbol carriers/escorts.
- **Positioning:** Rushes the nearest capsule/relic node, then contests nodes the player owns.
- **Defend:** When it stands on a node, so it keeps holding it.
- **Skill:** Tidal Pull (pull a holder off a node), Astral Shift (teleport onto a node).
- **Tuning knob:** `node_value = 3x a unit KO`

### long-range sniper (x12)

Stages: T01-L08, T02-L05, T03-L02, T04-L09, T05-L06, T06-L03, T07-L10, T08-L07, T09-L04, T10-L01, T11-L08, T12-L05

- **Target priority:** Highest damage-per-hit target it can reach without moving into enemy reach.
- **Positioning:** Keeps distance >= its range and outside player move+range. Prefers elevation and LOS lanes behind obstacles/stone.
- **Defend:** When it cannot shoot this turn without stepping into danger.
- **Skill:** Damage amplifiers (Ember Mark, Chain Spark, Moon Pulse) when 2+ targets cluster.
- **Tuning knob:** `kite_margin = 1 tile`

### status-effect strategist (x12)

Stages: T01-L09, T02-L06, T03-L03, T04-L10, T05-L07, T06-L04, T07-L01, T08-L08, T09-L05, T10-L02, T11-L09, T12-L06

- **Target priority:** The highest-power player unit (disable the carry).
- **Positioning:** Mid-range: stays within skill range of 2+ targets.
- **Defend:** Rarely. Only when all its skills are on cooldown and it is threatened.
- **Skill:** Always uses an available control skill (Frost Lock via crit-fishing, Gravity Well, Tidal Pull, Ember Mark) before plain attacks.
- **Tuning knob:** `skill_bias = 1.0 (always if available)`

### rush attacker (x12)

Stages: T01-L10, T02-L07, T03-L04, T04-L01, T05-L08, T06-L05, T07-L02, T08-L09, T09-L06, T10-L03, T11-L10, T12-L07

- **Target priority:** Shortest BFS path to the player Symbol. It attacks units only if they block that path or can be KO'd this turn.
- **Positioning:** Full move toward the player Symbol every turn, all units together.
- **Defend:** Never.
- **Skill:** Offensive skills only (Moon Pulse, Chain Spark, Storm Dash chains).
- **Tuning knob:** `symbol_focus = 0.8`

Shared AI scoring for every style: `score(action) = w_dmg*expected_damage + w_ko*P(KO) + w_obj*objective_value - w_risk*incoming_threat_next_round`. Each style above only reweights these terms. Bosses should add one boss-only behaviour on top of their style: at <= 50% HP, switch to the 'rush attacker' targeting for one round (boss_phase dialogue fires).

## 7. Special rules

4 distinct special rules:

| rule | stages | level slots | implementable definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrain bonuses are doubled | 36 | L01 L05 L09 | Every POSITIVE terrain modifier is doubled for both sides: high ground becomes +60% damage, +30 accuracy, +2 range, and terrain-family match becomes +40% damage dealt / -20% taken. Penalties (water move cost, water/hazard damage) are unchanged. Implement as `terrainBonusMult = 2` applied to the bonus delta, not to the total. |
| Movement after a knockout is +1 | 36 | L02 L06 L10 | When a unit KOs an enemy (or the Symbol takes its final hit), that unit gets `move + 1` on its NEXT turn only, for both sides. It does not stack with itself; multiple KOs in one turn still give +1. It stacks with Storm Dash, which grants an immediate extra move. |
| One random portal activates each round | 24 | L03 L07 | At the start of each round, pick one `warp_gate` tile uniformly at random (seeded RNG). A unit that ENTERS the active gate may teleport, as a free action, to any other warp gate or, if the map has only one gate, to the mirrored tile `(11-r, 11-c)` (or the nearest free walkable tile to it). The active gate glows. Inactive gates are plain ground. |
| Vessels cost +1 SP to crack | 24 | L04 L08 | Deploying from a capsule costs 1 AP from the side's pool (see Action points) in addition to the unit's action. With 2 AP per round, at most 2 deploys per round. When this rule is absent, deploys cost 0 AP. |

Data support: the portal rule appears on 24 stages, which have Counter({1: 24}) warp gates (count: stages). With one gate it can't pair, hence the mirrored-tile fallback above. Better: add a second gate. 'Terrain bonuses are doubled' runs on maps where 120/120 have at most 1 elevation tile, so it is mostly a no-op until terrain is added (see section 9).

## 8. Win conditions

| win condition | stages | implementable definition |
|---|---|---|
| Defeat the enemy Symbol | 84 | Victory when the enemy Symbol's HP reaches 0. Defeat when the player Symbol's HP reaches 0 OR all player units are KO'd with no capsules left. Wiping the enemy roster does not win by itself: the Symbol must still fall, but with no defenders it takes no extra rules. |
| Defeat the enemy Symbol or secure 3 relic nodes | 36 | Either the Symbol condition above, OR at the start of the player's round the player OWNS 3 nodes at once. A node (`relic` or `capsule_node` tile, because each map has only 1 relic, see the integrity section) is owned by the last side to end a unit's turn on it with no enemy unit adjacent. Same defeat condition. The enemy AI can also win by securing 3 nodes. |

Relic stages have relic + capsule node counts of {3: 36}. Counting capsule nodes as relic nodes makes '3 nodes' reachable on 36/36 relic stages, but with zero slack on those with exactly 3 (36): the player must hold every node at once. Adding a 4th node gives the condition some play.

Owner's multi-objectives, mapped onto the same machinery:

| objective | definition |
|---|---|
| defeat all | Optional/secondary: if every enemy unit is KO'd and no enemy capsules remain, the enemy Symbol loses its DEF (becomes 0). |
| protect the Player Sigil | Already the loss condition of every stage (player Symbol HP 0 = defeat). |
| activate capsule nodes | Same ownership rule as relic nodes. An owned capsule node is a deploy point and restores 1 AP to its owner at round start. |
| relic | Optional scoring: owning the relic at battle end gives +1 reward tier. On relic-win stages it counts toward the 3 nodes. |

**Chokepoints depend on how `stone` is treated.** With stone walkable, the articulation-tile distribution is {0: 38, 1: 37, 2: 23, 3: 12, 4: 4, 5: 5, 7: 1} (38 maps have none). With stone blocking, it is {0: 33, 1: 35, 2: 24, 3: 11, 4: 6, 5: 6, 7: 2, 8: 1, 9: 1, 10: 1} (33 maps have none, mean 1.8). Of the 389 bridge tiles, 2 are true chokepoints (articulation vertices) with stone walkable and 4 with stone blocking. The owner's 'bridges are 1-wide chokepoints' rule mostly does not hold on these grids: almost no bridge tile is an articulation vertex, so units can walk around bridges. Flank bridges with obstacle/water tiles if they are meant to be chokepoints. Decide whether `stone` blocks movement before the map pass; it is the single biggest lever on board openness.

**Spawn template bias (systemic, all maps).** 112/120 maps use player spawns [(2, 2), (2, 3), (3, 2)] and enemy spawns [(9, 9), (9, 10), (10, 9)]. The player's spawns step toward the centre, the enemy's toward its corner, and 0/120 maps are point-symmetric. Result: raw capsule sums {-2: 107, -4: 3, -3: 7, 1: 3} and Symbol paths (player->enemy, enemy->player) {(15, 16): 107, (15, None): 7, (15, 17): 3, (15, 18): 2, (16, 16): 1}, so the player is about 1 tile closer to everything. With mirrored enemy spawns the capsule sums become {0: 110, -2: 7, 2: 2, 3: 1} and the paths {(15, 15): 110, (15, None): 7, (15, 17): 2, (16, 15): 1}. Fix: move the enemy spawns to the mirror of the player's, `(9,9), (9,8), (8,9)`. The per-map UNFAIR flags below use the corrected numbers.

The bible and PDF state no matchups (keyword search for 'weak to', 'beats', 'strong against' and 'super effective' finds nothing). The concept sheet only gives flavour words (Nature/Life, Fire/War, ...). The data has 16 elements in 12 families: 4 families hold 2 elements each (Fire=124, Water=126, Earth=124, Wind=125 monsters), and 8 hold 1. The proposal is **three rings of four**. In each ring a family beats the next one and loses to the previous one. Cross-ring matchups and opposite-in-ring matchups are neutral. Advantage is x1.25, disadvantage x0.8.

- **Elemental ring** (499 monsters): Fire > Wind > Earth > Water > Fire. *Fire burns away Wind, Wind erodes Earth, Earth dams Water, Water quenches Fire.*
- **Wild ring** (250 monsters): Lightning > Tech > Ice > Nature > Lightning. *Lightning shorts Tech, Tech's engines melt Ice, Ice freezes Nature, Nature's roots ground Lightning.*
- **Cosmic ring** (251 monsters): Light > Dark > Arcane > Space > Light. *Light banishes Dark, Dark corrupts Arcane, Arcane sigils bind Space, Space's gravity swallows Light.*

Why it works: every family has exactly ONE strength and ONE weakness, so the whole chart is 12 arrows a player can memorise as three loops. The 4 big families form the core ring, the one players meet first. Sub-elements inherit their family (Mist and Tide are both Water). Element multipliers apply after the damage floor fix (section 4). On the current formula they are invisible on 42.4% of pairs. Full matrix: `docs/data/element_chart.csv`.

## 11. Recommended starter squad

Picked by the script from Common/Uncommon only (363 monsters). Constraints: one Guardian, one Support and two other distinct roles; 4 distinct element families; at least 2 activatable abilities; at least one unit with range >= 3; no member with |power z| >= 1; no map-dependent ability that rarely fires (Rift Bite, Verdant Renewal, Soul Lantern; see ABILITIES.md). ## 12. Top 10 tuning changes

1. **Rarity multipliers** on hp/attack/defense: Common 1.00, Uncommon 1.06, Rare 1.12, Epic 1.20, Mythic 1.28, Legendary 1.36. Rarity currently explains 0.5% of power.
2. **Replace the damage formula** with `max(1, round(1.5*atk*atk/(atk+def)))`. This takes the 1-damage pairs from 42.4% to 0.0% and the median hits-to-KO from 41 to 6.
3. **Budget rosters by tower.** Target stage threat = T01 mean (15.9) x 1.12^(tower-1) x (1 + 0.03*(level-1)), with the boss at x1.3. Fill rosters by greedy pick from the monster pool, not ID windows (currently rho = +0.01).
4. **Make bosses bosses**: give the L10 opponent a unique boss monster (Legendary, 2x HP, 2 abilities) and restore the player Symbol on T01-L10, T03-L10, T05-L10, T07-L10, T08-L10, T09-L10, T11-L10.
5. **Relic win condition**: either place 3 relic tiles per relic stage (currently 1) or formally count capsule nodes as relic nodes (section 8).
6. **Portal rule**: place 2 warp gates on each of the 24 portal stages (currently 1 each), or use the mirrored-tile fallback.
7. **Decouple identity from ID**: reshuffle element/role/ability so each element spans 3+ roles (currently 1) and ability doesn't follow ID mod 20. Give Artillery range 4 and Supports range 3.
8. **Give every monster a Skill**: 650 monsters have only passive/triggered abilities. Add one shared active `Signature Strike` (x1.3 damage of own element, cooldown 2) for them. That is 1 extra script, not 13.
9. **Terrain that matters**: at least 3 elevation tiles and a themed terrain patch (>= 8 tiles of the tower's family) per map, and water on Water-tower maps. Today 12/120 maps have water and 120/120 have <= 1 elevation tile. Narrow the open boards: 38 maps have no chokepoint with stone walkable (33 with stone blocking), and only 2/389 bridge tiles are real chokepoints.
10. **Squad vs spawn count**: squad is 4, but maps give 3 spawn tiles and rosters are 5-7. Add a 4th player spawn, cap rosters at 4 on-field + capsules, fix the 483 JSON spawn coordinates so they match the grid, and mirror the enemy spawn template so the player stops starting 1 tile closer to every objective.
