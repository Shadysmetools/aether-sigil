# Aether Sigil: Abilities

Generated by `docs/tools/aether_report.py`. Counts come from `data/AETHER_SIGIL_MASTER_GAME_BIBLE.json`. Specs use the shared rule definitions in `BALANCE-REPORT.md` section 1 (accuracy 90%, crit 10% x1.5, round half up, min 1).

**20 distinct abilities** across 1000 monsters. Every ability has exactly 50 holders and 1 description text, so there are no near-duplicate NAMES to merge. Because of the ID-modulo generator, each ability sits on exactly 2 roles and 4 elements. Trigger split: passive 9, active 7, on-hit 2, on-crit 1, on-KO 1.

**Scripts needed:** 20 ability scripts. They are built on **17 shared primitives** (`status` x6, `damage_mod` x6, `aoe_select` x2, `tile_query` x2, `stat_mod` x2, `charge` x1, `heal` x1, `reflect` x1, `zone` x1, `displace` x1, `pathing_mod` x1, `extra_move` x1, `teleport` x1, `spawn_object` x1, `tile_effect` x1, `reveal` x1, `copy_skill` x1). Near-duplicate behaviours collapse into 6 shared hooks (below), so the real engineering load is about 17 primitives plus 20 thin configs. Recommended addition: 1 shared `Signature Strike` active for the 650 monsters (65.0%) whose ability is not activatable, otherwise their Skill command is empty.

## Near-duplicate groups (share one hook)

| group | abilities | shared implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Damage reducers | Metal Skin, Prism Guard, Iron Root | one `incomingDamageModifier(unit, hit)` hook with per-ability predicates (HP>50% / first-hit-per-round / stationary) |
| Conditional bonus damage | Rift Bite, Ember Mark | one `outgoingDamageModifier` hook; Ember Mark adds a status, Rift Bite a tile check |
| Movement restriction | Frost Lock, Gravity Well | shared ROOT/SLOW status; Gravity Well applies it from a zone |
| Bonus movement | Storm Dash, Phase Step | shared `grantExtraMove(unit, n)`; triggers differ (KO vs terrain change) |
| Hit riders | Chain Spark, Tidal Pull | shared `onHit` dispatcher; jump-damage vs displace |
| Tile/object creation | Crystal Bloom, Magma Trail | shared `TileEffect` entity with TTL |

## Damage

### Chain Spark

- **Data text:** A hit can jump to a second nearby enemy for reduced damage.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (36 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Summoner 25, Support 25. Elements: Crystal 12, Lumen 13, Metal 12, Terra 13.
- **Trigger:** on-hit (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** On a successful basic attack: a second hit jumps to the nearest other enemy within 2 tiles of the target (no LOS needed) for 50% of the dealt damage (min 1). Once per attack.
- **Primitives:** `damage_mod`, `aoe_select`

### Moon Pulse

- **Data text:** Charges over two turns, then emits a radial attack.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (36 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Summoner 25, Support 25. Elements: Crystal 12, Lumen 13, Metal 13, Terra 12.
- **Trigger:** active (usable via the Skill command)
- **Spec:** Skill: CHARGE (unit cannot move, Wait counts) for 2 of its own turns. On the 3rd turn it auto-fires: all enemies within radius 2 take x1.20 of base damage. Allies are unaffected. KO or displacement cancels the charge. Cooldown 3.
- **Primitives:** `charge`, `aoe_select`, `damage_mod`

### Rift Bite

- **Data text:** Deals more damage to enemies standing on portals or warp tiles.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (35 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Scout 25, Striker 25. Elements: Ember 13, Magma 13, Umbral 12, Verdant 12.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive: basic attacks against a unit standing on a `warp_gate` (or the active portal tile) deal x1.50. Also applies to enemies on `rift` tiles if they are added.
- **Primitives:** `damage_mod`
- **Map dependency:** needs a unit on a warp tile: 118/120 maps have any warp_gate, and every one of them has exactly 1 (out of 144 tiles).


## Heal

### Verdant Renewal

- **Data text:** Restores a small amount of HP when ending a turn on Nature terrain.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (40 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Scout 25, Striker 25. Elements: Ember 12, Magma 12, Umbral 13, Verdant 13.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive: at end of own turn on a Nature tile (`forest`, or any tile on a Nature-theme stage), heal 8% max HP (round up).
- **Primitives:** `heal`, `tile_query`
- **Map dependency:** needs Nature terrain: 12/120 maps have any `forest` tile (122 forest tiles in total). The rule falls back to Nature-theme stages (10).


## Buff

### Iron Root

- **Data text:** Cannot be displaced by knockback and gains defense while stationary.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (37 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Assassin 25, Controller 25. Elements: Aether 13, Gale 12, Storm 13, Volt 12.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive: immune to displacement (Tidal Pull, pushes). If it did not move this turn, +20% DEF until its next turn (shown as a stance icon).
- **Primitives:** `status`, `stat_mod`
- **Map dependency:** its knockback clause only matters against Tidal Pull, the only displacement effect in the data.

### Lumen Ward

- **Data text:** Protects an adjacent ally from the next status effect.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (35 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Artillery 25, Guardian 25. Elements: Astral 13, Frost 13, Mist 12, Tide 12.
- **Trigger:** active (usable via the Skill command)
- **Spec:** Skill: adjacent ally (or self) gains WARD. It cancels the next status (Mark, Root, Slow, Pull) applied to it. Lasts until consumed or 3 turns. Cooldown 2.
- **Primitives:** `status`

### Metal Skin

- **Data text:** Reduces physical damage while HP is above half.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (37 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Assassin 25, Controller 25. Elements: Aether 12, Gale 13, Storm 12, Volt 13.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive: while HP > 50% max, incoming damage from basic attacks is x0.75 (applied before the min-1 floor is re-applied).
- **Primitives:** `damage_mod`

### Mist Veil

- **Data text:** Becomes harder to target until it attacks.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (32 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Artillery 25, Guardian 25. Elements: Astral 12, Frost 12, Mist 13, Tide 13.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive: VEILED (attackers -30 accuracy) at battle start and after every turn it does not attack. Attacking removes it until its next non-attacking turn.
- **Primitives:** `status`

### Prism Guard

- **Data text:** Reduces the first incoming hit each round and reflects a fraction of it.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (34 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Scout 25, Striker 25. Elements: Ember 12, Magma 12, Umbral 13, Verdant 13.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive: the first incoming hit each round is x0.70. 25% of the prevented amount (min 1) is dealt back to the attacker if adjacent or in range 1.
- **Primitives:** `damage_mod`, `reflect`


## Debuff

### Ember Mark

- **Data text:** Marks an enemy; the next attack against it deals bonus elemental damage.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (37 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Assassin 25, Controller 25. Elements: Aether 13, Gale 12, Storm 13, Volt 12.
- **Trigger:** active (usable via the Skill command)
- **Spec:** Skill: single enemy within range+1, LOS. Applies MARK (2 turns or until consumed). The next damaging hit on the target from any ally gets x1.30 and is typed as the marker's element. Cooldown 2.
- **Primitives:** `status`, `damage_mod`

### Frost Lock

- **Data text:** A critical hit briefly prevents the target from moving.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (36 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Assassin 25, Controller 25. Elements: Aether 12, Gale 13, Storm 13, Volt 12.
- **Trigger:** on-crit (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** On a critical basic attack: target is ROOTED (move = 0) for its next turn. Does not stack.
- **Primitives:** `status`

### Gravity Well

- **Data text:** Creates a temporary zone that reduces movement around the target tile.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (34 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Scout 25, Striker 25. Elements: Ember 12, Magma 13, Umbral 13, Verdant 12.
- **Trigger:** active (usable via the Skill command)
- **Spec:** Skill: target tile within range, LOS. Creates a 3x3 SLOW zone for 2 rounds. Units starting or entering inside have move -2 (min 1). Cooldown 3.
- **Primitives:** `zone`, `status`

### Tidal Pull

- **Data text:** Drags a nearby enemy one tile toward this unit after a successful hit.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (36 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Summoner 25, Support 25. Elements: Crystal 12, Lumen 13, Metal 13, Terra 12.
- **Trigger:** on-hit (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** On a successful basic attack at range >= 2: pull the target 1 tile toward this unit along the straight line if that tile is walkable and empty. No pull through obstacles. Iron Root/Defend/Ward resist.
- **Primitives:** `displace`
- **Map dependency:** water exists on only 12/120 maps, so pulling into water rarely applies.


## Movement

### Astral Shift

- **Data text:** Teleports to a visible unoccupied special node once per battle.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (33 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Summoner 25, Support 25. Elements: Crystal 13, Lumen 12, Metal 12, Terra 13.
- **Trigger:** active (usable via the Skill command)
- **Spec:** Skill (once per battle): teleport to any unoccupied special node (`capsule_node`, `relic`, `warp_gate`, `elevation`) in LOS or within 6 tiles. Counts as the unit's action. It may still Move before using it.
- **Primitives:** `teleport`, `tile_query`
- **Map dependency:** needs special nodes: 4.8 per map on average (capsule+relic+warp+elevation).

### Phase Step

- **Data text:** Ignores one obstacle and gains bonus movement after entering a new terrain type.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (39 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Artillery 25, Guardian 25. Elements: Astral 12, Frost 12, Mist 13, Tide 13.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive: pathing may pass through ONE `obstacle`/`stone` tile per move (cannot end on it). After entering a tile whose terrain type differs from the start tile, +1 move this turn (once).
- **Primitives:** `pathing_mod`, `stat_mod`
- **Map dependency:** needs an obstacle/terrain change. That is common: every map has obstacles.

### Storm Dash

- **Data text:** May move again after defeating an enemy, with reduced range.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (37 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Artillery 25, Guardian 25. Elements: Astral 12, Frost 13, Mist 12, Tide 13.
- **Trigger:** on-KO (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** On KO (by any damage this unit deals): may immediately move again up to floor(move/2) tiles. Once per turn.
- **Primitives:** `extra_move`


## Summon

### Crystal Bloom

- **Data text:** Creates a temporary defensive crystal on an adjacent tile.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (37 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Artillery 25, Guardian 25. Elements: Astral 13, Frost 12, Mist 13, Tide 12.
- **Trigger:** active (usable via the Skill command)
- **Spec:** Skill: place a CRYSTAL (blocks movement and LOS, 20% of caster max HP, DEF = caster DEF) on an adjacent empty tile for 3 rounds or until destroyed. Max 1 per caster. Cooldown 2.
- **Primitives:** `spawn_object`

### Magma Trail

- **Data text:** Leaves heated tiles behind while moving; enemies crossing them take damage.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (37 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Scout 25, Striker 25. Elements: Ember 13, Magma 12, Umbral 12, Verdant 13.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive: each tile it leaves while moving becomes HEATED for 2 rounds. An enemy entering or ending on a heated tile takes 6% of its max HP (once per tile per turn). Max 6 heated tiles per unit.
- **Primitives:** `tile_effect`


## Utility

### Soul Lantern

- **Data text:** Reveals hidden traps and concealed capsules within a short radius.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (33 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Assassin 25, Controller 25. Elements: Aether 13, Gale 12, Storm 12, Volt 13.
- **Trigger:** passive (no Skill-command use)
- **Spec:** Passive aura: reveals hidden traps and concealed capsules within radius 3 at the start of its turn (fog/stealth flag cleared for both sides' visibility).
- **Primitives:** `reveal`
- **Map dependency:** needs hidden traps: 60/120 stages list 'hidden trap'; no stage has concealed capsules.

### Void Echo

- **Data text:** Repeats a weakened version of the last ability used nearby.
- **Holders:** 50 monsters (39 enemy-roster slots). Roles: Summoner 25, Support 25. Elements: Crystal 13, Lumen 12, Metal 13, Terra 12.
- **Trigger:** active (usable via the Skill command)
- **Spec:** Skill: repeat the most recent ACTIVE skill used by any unit within 4 tiles in the last round, at 50% numbers (damage/heal/duration rounded down, min 1). It cannot echo Void Echo or Astral Shift. Cooldown 3.
- **Primitives:** `copy_skill`
